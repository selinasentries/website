CREATIVE PRACTICE

I’m Selly, a creative who enjoys exploring the meeting point between visual storytelling, thoughtful layouts, and simple design. I’m drawn to monochrome palettes, tactile textures, generous white space, and small details that make a piece feel considered.

This portfolio brings together selected projects and experiments—from editorial compositions to digital concepts. It’s a living collection, so it will keep growing as new work takes shape.

What I’m interested in

Building clear visual systems, making ideas feel tangible, and creating work that feels calm, intentional, and easy to connect with.
